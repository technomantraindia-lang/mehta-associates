Company Profile

A Heritage of Trust in Pharmaceutical Sourcing

Established in 1985, Mehta Associates is one of Mumbai's leading and reputed dealers in Pharmaceutical Raw Materials, Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), Bulk Drugs, and Specialty Chemicals.

Operating from Mumbai, India's premier commercial and chemical trading centre, we maintain direct distribution bridges with certified manufacturing plants, supplying formulation units, institutional hospitals, and healthcare facilities nationwide.

1st Generation • Established 1985

Late Shri Shashikant C Mehta

Founding Visionary (1985)

Late Shri Shashikant C Mehta founded Mehta Associates in 1985 as an entrepreneurial chemical and raw materials trading firm in Mumbai. Built upon core virtues of uncompromising integrity, technical authenticity, and client-first commitment, he laid the cornerstone for what has become an esteemed, multi-generational institutional supplier.

“That small tree planted by Late Shri Shashikant C Mehta in 1985 has grown into a respected, pan-India pharmaceutical distributor through the continuous dedication of his sons and third-generation leadership.” His timeless values continue to govern our ethical business conduct and customer stewardship every day.
