Company Profile

A Heritage of Trust in Pharmaceutical Sourcing

Established in 1985, Mehta Associates takes pleasure in introducing ourselves as one of the leading and reputed Pharmaceuticals Raw Materials, API Products, Bulk Drugs & Chemicals Dealers in the Indian market.

Operating from the financial hub of India, Mumbai, we have developed an excellent supplier network and established a customer base across diverse domestic formulation centres.

The belief that customer satisfaction is as important as their products and service has helped this establishment gain a vast base of customers, which continues to grow day by day. In the near future, our business aims to expand its line of products and cater to an even larger client base.
