☱ HOW WE DELIVER

A proven path to certified pharmaceutical purity.

Every consignment follows a structured 3-step quality and supply process that minimizes regulatory risk, verifies compendial compliance, and ensures seamless nationwide delivery.

Step 01
COMPENDIAL VERIFICATION

We verify pharmacopoeial monograph (I P / B P / U S P), CAS identity, and origin standards

Every raw material is systematically checked against official monograph standards before procurement to ensure exact chemical identity, assay potency, and strict regulatory conformance.

Step 02
BATCH CERTIFICATE OF ANALYSIS

Every lot is released with manufacturer COA detailing HPLC/GC assay and heavy metals

Full analytical testing documentation, physicochemical test results, residual solvent limits, and GMP compliance certificates accompany every dispatch for audit readiness.

Step 03
STRATEGIC MUMBAI LOGISTICS

Temperature-controlled warehousing in Mumbai & Bhiwandi ensuring rapid ex-stock dispatch

Strategic inventory reserves enable immediate delivery across India with tamper-evident packaging, secure chain of custody, and dedicated direct partner coordination.
